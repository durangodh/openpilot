https://github.com/flywithu/LightSignal

Model weights: AGPL-3.0, see LICENSE.
SHA-256: 458c8f1273ae407e6fa8334159a7f6dc29c88a2680b15eae2771105b0bdeffc2

https://universe.roboflow.com/min-yong-park/original-korean-traffic-light
